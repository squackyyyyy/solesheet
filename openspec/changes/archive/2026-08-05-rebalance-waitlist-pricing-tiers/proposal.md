## Why

The waitlist currently frames basic search and filtering as paid-plan value while undervaluing the Growth workflow. The pricing preview should keep the free product genuinely usable, make Starter's ₱99 value about protection and routine convenience, and price Growth at ₱349 for serious multi-surface inventory operations.

## What Changes

- Make search and filtering an explicit Free-plan capability alongside the core inventory, profit, installment, sold-history, and local-export workflows.
- Reframe Starter at ₱99/month around 150 active pairs, automatic cloud backup and recovery, installment reminders, and monthly business summaries.
- Change the planned Growth price from ₱179/month to ₱349/month and its annual direction to ₱3,490/year, while retaining its browser workflow, import, synchronization, and advanced-reporting differentiation.
- Update the pricing section headline and supporting copy so it communicates a useful free tier and paid value through protection, scale, and time savings.
- Keep every displayed plan price explicitly planned/validation-stage and non-purchasable.
