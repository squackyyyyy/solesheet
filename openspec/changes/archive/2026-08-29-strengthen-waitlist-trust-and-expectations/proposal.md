## Why

SoleSheet now presents a polished product and collects real waitlist interest, but several signals can still leave a first-time visitor uncertain about what joining guarantees, whether preview numbers are real results, and who stands behind the product. A focused trust pass can make the validation-stage offer feel more credible without adding another large landing-page section or overstating product readiness.

## What Changes

- Standardize every initial waitlist entry action around one clear promise and explain what happens after joining, what contact the visitor may receive, and what joining does not guarantee.
- Clarify that the founding Starter price remains a planned validation offer whose eligibility and redemption rules will be confirmed before launch; remove call-to-action wording that implies founding access is already guaranteed.
- Add visible sample-data disclosures to mock metrics and planned product previews that could otherwise be mistaken for customer results or live business data.
- Add a compact, owner-approved builder note explaining why SoleSheet is being built, without fabricating testimonials, customer counts, partnerships, or personal credentials.
- Surface the validation-stage and likely-first-platform status near the signup decision while continuing to welcome feedback from both Android and iPhone users.
- Replace prototype-like footer wording, publish the general branded contact, and use the support alias—not the privacy alias—for survey-response change requests.
- Preserve the existing landing-page sections, pricing research, waitlist persistence, automatic survey opening, and survey requirements.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `waitlist-landing-experience`: Aligns waitlist promises, preview disclosures, builder trust, launch status, and footer presentation.
- `validation-survey`: Routes submitted-response change requests through the appropriate branded support address.
- `branded-email-routing`: Gives each published alias a clear public purpose while retaining the existing forwarding-only routing model.

## Impact

- Landing-page CTA labels, nearby explanatory copy, pricing/founding-offer copy, sample preview labels, final trust content, and footer content.
- Shared site-content and branded contact constants used by the landing page and survey completion state.
- Landing, survey, privacy/contact, responsive, and accessibility assertions.
- No changes to D1 schema, waitlist or survey request payloads, consent scope, Cloudflare Email Routing rules, analytics collection, product pricing records, or outbound email behavior.
